# The American Environmental Economy and What It Really Costs

## The question

America says it wants a clean-energy future, but building the power lines, reactors, and factories that future requires has become slow, expensive, and tangled in legal challenges. This page asks what would actually have to change, in our laws, our institutions, and our culture, for the country to build a serious new environmental economy. It also asks the harder follow-up: if we clear the path to build faster, what protections or voices might we give up along the way?
