Come Get Your Gas, or I'll Light It on Fire

Leak it, lose it: foreclose on methane leaks, capture the giants, burn the rest

1

THE IDEA

Leaked methane becomes abandoned property. Fix your leak in 60 days, or the gas is foreclosed on and auctioned to someone who will use it. If nobody bids, we burn it: a burning leak is 8–30× better for the climate than a silent one.

2

THE PROBLEM

Oil, gas and coal leak 124 Mt of methane a year (IEA).

  • Escaped gas belongs to no one, so leaking is free.
  • ~30 Mt could be fixed at no net cost, and still isn't.
  • Fines just become a price.
3

NOT EVERY LEAK IS EQUAL

  • Small (~80% of sites, ~10–25% of methane): fails cost-benefit. Leave it.
  • Medium (~30–45% of methane): too small to sell. Burn it.
  • Super-emitters (~1–5% of sites, ~40–60% of methane): power a data center.
7

INCENTIVES & THE FENCE

  • Leaking now costs more than fixing, so most owners just fix it. The best foreclosure never happens.
  • The fence: escaped gas had no owner, cleanups meant inheriting liability, and leaks were invisible until satellites.
4

THE PLAN

  1. Detect: satellites flag the leak.
  2. Notice: the owner gets 60 days to fix it.
  3. Foreclose: the gas rights are auctioned; the owner pays for the fix.
  4. No bidder: burn it cleanly, far from homes.
5

THE NUMBERS

~45% of fossil methane stopped. ~54 billion m³ of gas recovered a year: enough for half of today's data centers.

Why data centers buy: moving data is cheaper than moving gas. A leak has no pipeline, but it can have an internet line.

6

PROOF IT WORKS

  • Nigeria auctioned flare gas at 49 sites to 42 bidders.
  • Crusoe ran ~125 data centers on waste gas, cutting methane ~98% vs. flaring.
  • Germany uses ~99% of its old-mine methane.
8

KEY CONCERNS

  • "That's a taking!" Owners keep all gas they capture.
  • Fake leaks: only leaks seen before a cutoff count.
  • Gas lock-in: no new drilling allowed.
  • Smoke: no open flares near homes.

BOTTOM LINE: Leaked gas is wasted money and wasted climate. Make leaking cost more than fixing, and owners fix it themselves. What they won't fix gets captured and used, and what nobody wants gets burned.
